Hint 1 — structure: use a single index i through three phases.
Phase 1: advance i past leading ' ' characters.
Phase 2: if s[i] is '+' or '-', record the sign and advance i once.
Anything else (like a second sign in "+-12") means the digits phase
will find no digits, which correctly yields 0.
Phase 3: consume digits from i onward, stopping at the first non-digit.
