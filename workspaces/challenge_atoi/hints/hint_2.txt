Hint 2 — reading a digit: a char is a digit when
c >= '0' && c <= '9'. Its numeric value is c - '0'.
Any other char ends the digits phase (break), even mid-string —
that is how "4193 with words" stops at the space and
"00000-42a1234" stops at '-'. Track whether you saw at least one
digit; if not, return 0.
